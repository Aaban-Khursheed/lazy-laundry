Pickup & Delivery Policy

This policy describes how pickup and delivery work for every Lazy Laundry booking. It applies together with the Terms of Service.

Service area

We collect and return laundry inside Edumetro, USJ 1 only: Block A/B, Block C, Block E, and Colonial. Bookings require a valid block and unit number.

Pickup windows

  • Every evening: 7:00 pm, 8:00 pm, and 9:00 pm (Malaysia time).
  • Saturday and Sunday mornings also: 10:00 am, 11:00 am, and 12:00 pm.
  • You can book up to 7 days ahead and at least 1 hour before the slot starts.
  • Each slot takes one booking. Taken slots are not offered at checkout.

Turnaround

  • Lazy Wash (standard): ready about 12 hours after pickup; evening pickups return the next morning (about 15 hours).
  • Lazy Wash Express: ready about 6 hours after pickup.
  • The ready time shown on your tracking page is the committed estimate for your booking.

On pickup day

  • Have your laundry counted and ready at your unit door at the start of your slot.
  • Folding and ironing are included. Hangers are an optional extra chosen at checkout.
  • Payment is made at pickup by cash or QR — the exact total is shown before you confirm and on your tracking page.

Changing or cancelling a booking

  • Reschedule, edit, or cancel free of charge until 10 minutes before your pickup starts, from your private tracking link.
  • Cancelling within 10 minutes of pickup adds a RM3.00 late-cancellation fee to your next booking.
  • If we arrive for pickup and the laundry is not there, the operator may record a no-show, which cancels the booking and adds a RM3.00 fee to your next booking.
  • Outstanding fees are added to your next booking total automatically and shown before you confirm.

Delivery

  • Clean laundry is returned to the same unit during an upcoming pickup window once marked ready.
  • Your tracking page shows each status change — pickup on the way, collected, processing, ready, delivered.

Questions

Reply to our WhatsApp message about your booking, or contact Lazy Laundry on WhatsApp with your booking number (LL-XXXXXXXX), for help with pickup timing, access instructions, or a missed collection.
