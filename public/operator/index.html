Workspace help

Customer details and downloads are private. Server authorization and audit history apply to every change.

Overall summary covers all database orders, regardless of queue filters. Confirmed awaits pickup; active is in progress. Payment due and collected exclude cancelled orders.

Search checks booking, customer, phone and location in the database. Pickup dates and times are Malaysia time. Refresh updates this page and the selected order without discarding a note draft.

CSV contains private customer data: up to 100 orders for the selected status and pickup date. Search and queue pagination do not apply to CSV exports.

Private tracking administration, notes, fees and exceptional actions are available in the selected order's expandable sections.
